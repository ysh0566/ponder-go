## Callback RPC methods

The Go starter passes an `*rpc.Client` to `runtime.Handler`. Generated callback registries expose an `*indexing.Client` with event-aware reads. The pinned Ponder callback API lists 21 named actions. All 21 have high-level methods on `indexing.Client`. | State and contract reads | `GetBalance`, `GetCode`, `GetStorageAt`, `ReadContract`, `Multicall`, `GetFeeHistory`, `GetProof`. Fee history and EIP-1186 proofs default to the event block. The lower-level `rpc.Client` has its own receipt helper. Native multicall resolves the chain’s Multicall3 deployment at the requested height or accepts an explicit address. A sender or state override applies to the whole aggregate. Per-contract caching applies to aggregates that use the ordinary sender and state. ## Historical runtime selections

The v14 selected plan is **132 groups, 669 unique qualified native leaves**
(456 pure, 144 PostgreSQL, 69 PGlite), plus **276 race executions**, for **945
case executions**. It reruns the entire v13 selection of 612 unique leaves on the
new input. Its 57 additional qualified leaves comprise 36 omnichain/receipt
supplements and 21 affected legacy store controls. These are selection counts,
not whole-project completion. See
[validation](https://github.com/ysh0566/ponder-go/blob/2762a6bf5a2fbf339604306fbc1eee6f8f5554d3/parity/runs/m45-omnichain-v14-20261103/VALIDATION.md).
